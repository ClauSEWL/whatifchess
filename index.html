How it plays

Fair chaos

One twist each

Pieces move like normal chess. There is no check and no checkmate: capture the enemy king to win.

Chaos, announced

Meteors, bombs and storms are shown at least one move before they matter. You see them coming, and so does the AI. It never peeks further.

The AI or a friend

Three AI levels, Rookie, Club and Master, that understand every twist. Or Pass & Play on one phone.
