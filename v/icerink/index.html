Experiment 09 · Ice Rink Chess

What if the chessboard was frozen solid?

  1. Rooks, bishops, queens and kings slide all the way.
  2. They stop at the edge, before a friend, or crash into an enemy and take it.
  3. Only knights and pawns keep their footing.

Full rules

The twist

  1. Bishops, rooks, queens and the king can't stop where they like. In the chosen direction they slide all the way: to the edge, to just before one of your pieces, or into the first enemy piece, which they capture.
  2. Knights and pawns move normally. Castling is normal.

Base rules (every experiment)

  • Pieces move like normal chess.
  • No check, no checkmate: capture the enemy king to win.
  • Castling only needs an unmoved king and rook and an empty path.
  • 50 rounds without a capture, pawn move or destruction? The bigger army wins (equal armies: draw). Same after 150 rounds.

Inspired by Ice Chess by Zachary Morris.
