Experiment 07 · Fuse Chess

What if a queen and a knight fused into one monster?

  1. Instead of moving, slide a piece onto a friend right next to it.
  2. Knight + bishop, knight + rook, bishop + rook, knight + queen.
  3. Lose a fused piece and you lose both halves.

Full rules

The twist

  1. Instead of a move, step a knight, bishop, rook or queen one square (like a king) onto a neighbouring piece of yours and fuse them. Four recipes:
  2. Knight + Bishop = Archbishop (moves like a knight or a bishop). Knight + Rook = Chancellor (knight or rook). Bishop + Rook = Queen. Knight + Queen = Amazon (queen or knight).
  3. Pawns and kings don't fuse. A fused piece can't fuse again. Promoted pieces can fuse.
  4. A captured fused piece takes both halves with it. A rook that fuses loses castling on its side.

Base rules (every experiment)

  • Pieces move like normal chess.
  • No check, no checkmate: capture the enemy king to win.
  • Castling only needs an unmoved king and rook and an empty path.
  • 50 rounds without a capture, pawn move or destruction? The bigger army wins (equal armies: draw). Same after 150 rounds.
