Experiment 03 · Battle Royale Chess

What if chess was a battle royale?

  1. The storm shows the next safe zone.
  2. A few moves later, everything outside is eliminated, kings included.
  3. Two edge files, then both back ranks, then a 4×4 arena.

Full rules

The twist

  1. The storm announces the next safe zone with a glowing outline and closes a few moves later.
  2. Any piece outside the zone is eliminated, kings included, and those squares are gone for the rest of the game: nothing enters or passes through them.
  3. Stage 1 (announced after Black's 5th move, closes after White's 8th): 2 edge files vanish, split at random between left and right.
  4. Stage 2 (announced after Black's 14th move, closes after Black's 18th): both back ranks vanish.
  5. Stage 3 (announced after Black's 25th move, closes after White's 30th): the final arena, a 4×4 on ranks 3–6 at random files inside what is left.
  6. The zone is always fair: White and Black lose the same ranks, and who moves last before a close alternates.

Base rules (every experiment)

  • Pieces move like normal chess.
  • No check, no checkmate: capture the enemy king to win.
  • Castling only needs an unmoved king and rook and an empty path.
  • 50 rounds without a capture, pawn move or destruction? The bigger army wins (equal armies: draw). Same after 150 rounds.
