What If Chess: Crazy Variants

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026. Applies to the What If Chess: Crazy Variants app for iPhone and to this website.

The short version

  • The app collects no personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • Your settings, game statistics and your answer to "Where did you hear about us?" are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • The app never asks to track you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt) and contains no third-party SDKs.

1. Who we are

What If Chess: Crazy Variants ("the app", "we") is made by an independent developer, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: claudiutigaroi@gmail.com.

2. What stays on your iPhone

The app saves these on your device so it works the way you left it. They are never sent to us or to anyone else:

WhatWhy
Settings: sound, music, haptics, board, piece set, languageKeep your choices

Your answer to "Where did you hear about us?" (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, a friend, the App Store or somewhere else)Kept on the device only. We do not receive it
Onboarding and offer state (tutorial done, offer shown)So the app does not repeat them
Which Lab Pass products you own, as reported by AppleUnlock your experiments, also offline
A copy of the app's configuration file, if one was downloaded (see section 4)Use it offline

3. What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, address or date of birth.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.
  • No advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No payment details.

4. Network connections

  • The App Store. Buying, restoring and checking Lab Pass go through Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone. Apple processes these under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Configuration file. The app may download a small static file from this website (hosted on GitHub Pages) that tells it which experiment to feature and similar settings. The request contains no personal information and no identifier. Like any web host, GitHub processes your IP address to deliver the file (GitHub Privacy Statement). We do not receive or log it.
  • Links. Links to this website or to Apple open in Safari, where those sites' policies apply.

5. This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other companies' servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes your IP address to deliver the pages.

The TestFlight list. If you join the list, your email app opens a message to us with the email address you typed, the phone type you chose and the page or campaign you came from. Nothing is sent until you press send. We use them only to send you the TestFlight invite and news about the launch. We do not sell or share them. We delete them when you unsubscribe or ask us to, and at the latest one year after the launch. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw at any time by writing to claudiutigaroi@gmail.com.

App Store links on this site may contain a campaign token (for example ct=tiktok-meteor). Apple uses it to count, in aggregate, how many people downloaded the app from that link. We never see who you are.

6. Children

What If Chess: Crazy Variants is a general-audience board game rated 9+. It has no ads, no chat, no user-generated content and collects no personal data, from children or anyone else.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or US states such as California), you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data and to object to its use. The app holds nothing about you on our side; to remove what is on your iPhone, delete the app. For the TestFlight list, email claudiutigaroi@gmail.com and we answer within one month. You may also complain to your data-protection authority.

8. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, say so in the app or on this page. Questions: claudiutigaroi@gmail.com.
