How to play

Every experiment uses the same base rules:

  • Pieces move like normal chess.
  • No check, no checkmate: capture the enemy king to win.
  • Castling only needs an unmoved king and rook and an empty path.
  • 50 rounds without a capture, pawn move or destruction? The bigger army wins (equal armies: draw). Same after 150 rounds.

Then each one adds a twist. Tap an experiment in the app for a live demo and the full rules.

Hot Potato Chess

What if your pieces had to pass a ticking bomb?

  1. A bomb drops in the center. Step on it and you're holding it.
  2. End your move next to enemies and it jumps to the best one. Kings first.
  3. After ten moves it blows up everything around it.

Meteor Chess

What if meteors fell on your chessboard?

  1. About every three rounds, three squares light up red.
  2. Each side gets exactly one move to react. The showers take turns on who goes first.
  3. Then the meteors hit. Anything there is gone, kings included.

Battle Royale Chess

What if chess was a battle royale?

  1. The storm shows the next safe zone.
  2. A few moves later, everything outside is eliminated, kings included.
  3. Two edge files, then both back ranks, then a 4×4 arena.

Laser Rook Chess

What if rooks had lasers?

  1. From move five, charge a rook instead of moving it. Everyone sees the beam.
  2. Your opponent gets one move: dodge, block with two pieces, or take the rook.
  3. Zap. The beam passes your pieces and destroys the first two enemies.

Dragon Egg Chess

What if a dragon egg hatched on your chessboard?

  1. Hold more of the four center squares, even after your opponent replies.
  2. The egg warms toward you and hatches at 4. It starts one notch toward Black, who moves second.
  3. Your dragon moves like a queen and a knight. Pawns can't touch it.

Splat Chess

What if chess pieces left paint everywhere they went?

  1. Every unpainted square a piece crosses or lands on takes its color. Forever.
  2. Rooks and bishops paint whole lines. Knights splash only where they land.
  3. Capture the king, or own more of the board after 20 rounds.

Fuse Chess

What if a queen and a knight fused into one monster?

  1. Instead of moving, slide a piece onto a friend right next to it.
  2. Knight + bishop, knight + rook, bishop + rook, knight + queen.
  3. Lose a fused piece and you lose both halves.

Rock Paper Scissors Chess

What if every chess piece was rock, paper or scissors?

  1. Every piece but the king gets rock, paper or scissors, mirrored for both sides.
  2. You can't capture a piece whose sign beats yours.
  3. Kings have no sign: anyone can take them.

Ice Rink Chess

What if the chessboard was frozen solid?

  1. Rooks, bishops, queens and kings slide all the way.
  2. They stop at the edge, before a friend, or crash into an enemy and take it.
  3. Only knights and pawns keep their footing.

Ring-Out Chess

What if you could shove chess pieces off the board?

  1. Move into an enemy and you shove it one square back. No captures.
  2. Lines slide like dominoes. Whatever leaves the board is out.
  3. First to push out six wins. No ring-out for 20 rounds? The ring shrinks.

Questions

What is free?

Hot Potato Chess at the Rookie and Club levels and in Pass & Play, the tutorial, and the rules of every experiment.

What does Lab Pass include?

Every experiment, the Master level everywhere, premium piece sets and new experiments as they arrive in app updates. $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year (US prices). Every experiment can be tried in its free interactive tutorial. Family Sharing: up to six family members.

How do I restore my purchase on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the app, tap the gear on Home (Settings) and tap Restore Purchases. The paywall has the same button. Family members get Lab Pass the same way.

How do I cancel Lab Pass?

In the app: Settings › Lab Pass › Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › What If Chess: Crazy Variants. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. You keep Lab Pass until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made with Apple, so refunds are too: reportaproblem.apple.com.

I lost and I don't know why.

Before you confirm a move (tap it again), the board previews what it will cause: the blast, the laser's path, where the bomb jumps, the paint. "King in danger" warns you when your king can be taken next move. Events (meteors, the storm) are always announced at least one move ahead. Undo takes a move back: as often as you like at Rookie, once per game at Club, never at Master.

Are new experiments coming?

Yes. New experiments arrive in app updates and are included in Lab Pass. We don't announce dates.

Does it work offline?

Yes, everything except buying or restoring Lab Pass, which needs the App Store.

How do I change the language, sound or board?

Settings (the gear on Home): sound effects, music, haptics, each experiment's own board or the Classic Walnut board, piece sets, and English or Romanian.

What data do you collect?

None. Your stats and settings stay on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
